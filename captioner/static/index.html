Put Hinglish captions on your Reels, spelled the way people actually talk.

Drop a clip, fix the lines, pick a look, and burn them in. It runs on your Mac — the video never leaves it; only the audio goes to Groq to transcribe.

Groq API key

Captions are made with Groq (whisper-large-v3, then an LLM that cleans the Hinglish). It runs in the cloud, so the audio leaves your Mac. Get a free key — no card — at console.groq.com → API Keys. It's stored only in this browser and sent only to Groq.
